Auditory EEG Bench (AEB)

A fixed benchmark for naturalistic auditory EEG decoding, not an encoder paper. v0.1: six-dataset cache, listener-aware splits, leakage audit, frozen-probe and supervised reference results.

Scope

Target task: NMED-T 10-way, per-subject trial-wise 50/50. Training cache: six datasets, 19 channels, 200 Hz, 8-s windows (69,108 windows, 153.6 h). Raw EEG, audio and checkpoints are not redistributed. Random-row and block splits are controls; listener-held-out and leave-one-dataset-out are the stress tests.

v0.1 status

Reference frozen-probe results: band-power .840, six-dataset encoder .607, BIOT .381, LaBraM .358, CBraMod .250. Supervised baselines are fixed-budget reference points. The unified 3-seed fine-tune grid, tuned supervised budget and full LODO leaderboard are release blockers before v1.0.

No raw EEG, audio, or model checkpoints are published in this repository.
